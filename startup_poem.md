# A Poem on Startups in AI

In silicon halls and brainstormed skies,
A venture whispers, 'Let there be light.'
From dreams of algorithms, brave new codes,
The dawn comes early for the digital breeds.

Startups dance beneath the neon moons,
On cloud9s they aim to launch their tunes.
With data as the fuel that propels them higher,
The journey's fraught with tests and cries of 'Die hard!'
In labs, tech glows and theories interwreathe;
Yet risks are great, and visions sometimes cease.

Silent struggles in code's dense embrace,
A leap in the dark, the path to grace.
For every loss, an ocean of startups rise,
Each wave breaking, giving hope a prize.

With AI on watch, predictions made,
The future stirs in coded shard.
A dance with fate, through binary and fate,
In every start, one step in the dark we take.
